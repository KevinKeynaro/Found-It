# Found It!

## About

Lost & Found is the official school website for finding lost belongings. When an item is found on campus, it is handed to the teacher in charge of lost items, who posts it on the website with a photo and details. Students can then browse the list and check whether something they lost is there.

The goal is simple: make it faster and easier for students to get their belongings back, instead of relying on word of mouth or items piling up in a drawer.

## Who Uses It

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Manages user accounts and oversees all posted items. |
| **Teacher** | The teacher in charge of lost items. Posts found items with a photo, and edits or removes their own posts. |
| **Student** | Browses and searches the list of found items to look for their lost belongings. |

## How It Works

1. A student or staff member finds an item and hands it to the designated teacher.
2. The teacher logs in and posts the item with a title, description, where it was found, the date, and a photo.
3. Students log in and browse the list of found items.
4. When the owner is found, the item is marked as returned and removed or archived.

## Main Features

- Login with role-based access (Admin, Teacher, Student)
- Post, view, edit, and delete found items
- Upload a photo for each item
- Browse and search the list of found items
